# WealthFlow demonstration walkthrough

1. Launch the app, advance through onboarding, and show the role selector.
2. One-tap login as Distributor; point out data freshness, business metrics, AUM chart and demonstration disclaimer.
3. Open a dashboard alert, then use Advisor Action Centre to resolve an item and show local status feedback.
4. Open Clients, search for Rahul Mehta, and enter Client 360.
5. Show explainable portfolio movement: market movement separately from net cash flow.
6. Open SIP management, choose a failed SIP, confirm Retry, and show its updated local status.
7. Open the transaction wizard and proceed through suitability, review, confirmation and the simulated order timeline.
8. Open Goals, create a mock goal, and emphasize that projections are estimates.
9. Generate a proposal, preview it, simulate PDF generation and simulate sharing.
10. Open Data Imports and run success, warning and failure WBR scenarios. On failure, show error, retry and quarantine controls.
11. Open Reconciliation, resolve a unit mismatch, and mention the simulated audit entry.
12. Open the floating Demo AI assistant and ask why Rahul’s portfolio changed.
13. Log out and one-tap login as Client; show the simpler wealth, portfolio, SIP and goal experience.
14. Log out and login as Operations; show import health, reconciliation and provider freshness.
15. Log out and login as Administrator; show users, providers, governance controls, security and demo-data reset.
16. Toggle dark mode and resize Chrome to 320, 375, 390, 430 and tablet widths.
